Florida's new stem cell law (SB 1768, eff. 7/1/25) lets MDs/DOs perform unapproved stem cell therapy — but only for orthopedics, wound care, or pain management. Cognitive and neuro conditions aren't on the list. Silence in a statute isn't permission.

Source: flsenate.gov/Session/Bill/2025/1768/BillText/er/HTML, §458.3245(3)(a)
