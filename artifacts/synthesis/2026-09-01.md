# Synthesis

## What changed recently

Since the last synthesis (2026-08-31): every agent brief now warns against trusting its own prior conclusions as settled; formulation and spec were rewritten active-agnostic around an explicit four-axis criterion — mechanism, human-relevant evidence, manufacturability, regulatory tractability, mechanism weighted as a gate; a new challenge agent now attacks each day's artifact before this synthesis runs; and the disclosure standard and this agent's own brief were reframed around a "common language for approval" mission. Formulation then re-scored [`active-candidates.md`](active-candidates.md) under the new criterion, and [`challenges.md`](challenges.md) ran its first pass against that output.

## What we know

SGL-001's published spec still doesn't reconcile with itself or precedent — dose, container arithmetic, and "sterile filtration" as previously recorded ([`sgl-001-spec.md`](sgl-001-spec.md)). The cited legal basis still doesn't cover the marketed use ([`jurisdiction-map.md`](jurisdiction-map.md)), and none of the seven CDMO-readiness questions is answerable from public materials ([`cdmo-readiness.md`](cdmo-readiness.md)). No audited vendor meets the disclosure standard's minimum tier ([`signal-standard.md`](signal-standard.md)); the standard is now framed explicitly as the seed of a category-wide common language for approval, with manufacturing consistency and a defined mechanism named as the actual bottleneck, not a checklist detail.

Under the new criterion, **the current lead active is intranasal insulin, not extracellular vesicles** — strong on mechanism, evidence, manufacturability, and regulatory tractability; naive EVs score weakest of any candidate on three of the four axes, their mechanism undercut by an unresolved miRNA-stoichiometry problem ([`active-candidates.md`](active-candidates.md), [`mirna-cargo.md`](mirna-cargo.md)). This directly contradicts the product's current "nose signals the brain to repair itself" marketing, reported as such rather than softened.

The first challenge pass held that direction but not all its support: three of insulin's four axis-scores rested on general knowledge rather than a source checked this run, and the EV mechanism verdict was argued from only the miRNA-transfer pathway ([`challenges.md`](challenges.md)).

## What we don't know

Source cell line, dose basis, release analytics, storage, shelf life, device, and federal regulatory pathway remain open ([`cdmo-readiness.md`](cdmo-readiness.md), [`sgl-001-spec.md`](sgl-001-spec.md)). Whether SB 1768 covers exosomes at all is unresolved ([`jurisdiction-map.md`](jurisdiction-map.md)). Whether insulin's manufacturability and regulatory scores survive an actual primary-source check, and whether EVs have a non-miRNA mechanism case, are both now open per challenge's findings ([`challenges.md`](challenges.md)). Whether the operator resolves the product-identity tension toward the evidence (insulin) or the current marketed identity (EVs) is unknown and not this synthesis's call.

## What it means for the product

For the first time, the artifact record names a lead active that isn't the one being marketed — a direct instance of the defined-mechanism problem the mission reframe names as central, not incidental. None of this is evaluated here as good or bad; it is the state of the record, now including an unresolved product-identity question alongside the pre-existing spec, legal, and manufacturing gaps.
