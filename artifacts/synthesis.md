# Synthesis

## What changed recently

Since the last synthesis (earlier the same day): the product site moved from spray.signal.clinic to sgl001.signal.clinic, and on an operator report — verified directly against the live page before editing anything — three spec conflicts and the jurisdiction conflict are now resolved: the dose is labelled a development target with an on-site disclaimer; no specific dose count is stated; "0.22 µm filtration" is labelled a process step, not a quality attribute; and Florida SB 1768 is no longer cited as the legal basis for launch ([`sgl-001-spec.md`](sgl-001-spec.md), [`jurisdiction-map.md`](jurisdiction-map.md)). All four are recorded as resolved, not deleted.

## What we know

Three of `sgl-001-spec.md`'s four self-published conflicts are now resolved by site correction. Storage temperature and shelf life remain unpublished — the one open spec conflict now. The dose-vs-precedent comparison stays as a note, not a conflict: 5×10⁹ is still 6.25× the top arm of the only human intranasal EV trial (n=9, uncontrolled) and 62.5× its concentration, on an acknowledged development target.

SB 1768 is no longer cited on the site at all; the underlying statutory-scope question (its indication list excludes cognition, and its product-type exclusion may not cover exosomes) stands as reference rather than a live conflict with Signal's own materials ([`jurisdiction-map.md`](jurisdiction-map.md)). The site's silence on federal law, IND status, and FDA review is unchanged and remains the one open jurisdiction conflict.

None of the seven CDMO-readiness questions is answerable from public materials ([`cdmo-readiness.md`](cdmo-readiness.md)). No audited vendor meets the disclosure standard's minimum tier, now framed as the seed of a category-wide common language for approval ([`signal-standard.md`](signal-standard.md)).

Unaffected by today's corrections: the four-axis criterion still names intranasal insulin, not extracellular vesicles, as the current lead active — naive EVs score weakest on three of four axes ([`active-candidates.md`](active-candidates.md)). The homepage still markets SGL-001 as "a biologic that signals the brain to repair itself," so that tension stands. Challenge's first pass held the lead's direction but flagged unsourced axis-scores and a narrow EV-mechanism argument ([`challenges.md`](challenges.md)).

## What we don't know

Source cell line, dose basis, release analytics, storage, shelf life, and device remain open ([`sgl-001-spec.md`](sgl-001-spec.md), [`cdmo-readiness.md`](cdmo-readiness.md)). Whether an exosome product falls under SB 1768 at all is still open, independent of what the site currently claims ([`jurisdiction-map.md`](jurisdiction-map.md)). Whether insulin's manufacturability and regulatory scores survive a primary-source check, and whether EVs have a non-miRNA mechanism case, are both still open ([`challenges.md`](challenges.md)). Whether the operator resolves the marketed-identity-vs-evidence tension is unknown — today's corrections were about accuracy, not about which active the product is built on.

## What it means for the product

The record now shows the operator actively correcting self-contradictions and an unsupported legal citation on the public site — recorded as a good-faith move toward accuracy, not evaluated further. The harder, unresolved items are unaffected: what the product's active actually is, its manufacturing basis, and whether its marketed mechanism holds up against the finding that a non-regenerative active currently scores best on the product's own selection criterion.
